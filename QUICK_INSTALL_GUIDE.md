# 🖥️ Quick Install Guide - Desktop App

## Install in 3 Simple Steps

### Chrome or Edge (Recommended) 👍

\`\`\`
Step 1: Open the app in Chrome/Edge browser
        👉 http://localhost:3000 or your app URL

Step 2: Look for install prompt
        • Banner appears automatically, or
        • Click ⋮ menu → "Install MDRRMO Pio Duran"

Step 3: Click "Install" button
        ✅ Done! App opens in its own window
\`\`\`

### Firefox 🦊

\`\`\`
Step 1: Open the app in Firefox

Step 2: Click the ⊕ icon in address bar
        OR click ☰ menu → "Install MDRRMO Pio Duran"

Step 3: Confirm installation
        ✅ Done!
\`\`\`

### Safari (Mac) 🍎

\`\`\`
Step 1: Open the app in Safari

Step 2: Click Share button (📤) → "Add to Dock"

Step 3: Confirm
        ✅ Done! App appears in Dock
\`\`\`
